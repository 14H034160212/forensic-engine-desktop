# Demo Taxonomy of Learning Capabilities

> **This is a small, generic DEMO taxonomy** shipped so the Learning DRE runs out of the box.
> It is illustrative only. To use the DRE for real, upload your own full taxonomy in the same format.
>
> **Design maxim:** Reason broadly · Report selectively · Preserve everything underneath.
> A large taxonomy is a search landscape, not a checklist — most constructs will have no opportunity
> in a single episode, and that is a valid, expected result.

## Controlled vocabulary

### COV.RELEVANT_FOUND
**Label:** Concrete evidence in this episode demonstrates the construct.

### COV.RELEVANT_INSUFFICIENT
**Label:** The construct is touched, but the evidence is too thin to judge.

### COV.NO_OPPORTUNITY
**Label:** Nothing in this episode created an opportunity to observe the construct.

### COV.NOT_RELEVANT
**Label:** The construct does not apply to this kind of episode.

### COV.NO_MATERIAL_FINDING
**Label:** Considered, but nothing worth reporting.

### JDG.CONCERN
**Label:** The evidence suggests a difficulty worth a teacher's attention.

### JDG.MIXED
**Label:** The evidence points both ways.

### JDG.SUPPORTIVE
**Label:** The evidence indicates a capability is present.

### JDG.STRONG
**Label:** The evidence indicates the capability is clearly demonstrated.

# D01 — Agency & Self-Direction

**Question:** Did the learner drive their own learning, or was it driven for them?

## D01.INITIATIVE
**Name:** Taking initiative
**Era:** durable
**Guidepost:** The learner starts, steers or extends the task without being told to at each step.

## D01.GOAL_SETTING
**Name:** Setting a learning goal
**Era:** durable
**Guidepost:** The learner names what they are trying to understand or produce.

## D01.HELP_SEEKING
**Name:** Productive help-seeking
**Era:** AI-era
**Guidepost:** The learner asks for help in a way that advances understanding rather than outsourcing it.

## D01.PERSISTENCE
**Name:** Persistence through difficulty
**Era:** durable
**Guidepost:** The learner keeps working on a point they did not immediately grasp.

## D01.SELF_CORRECTION
**Name:** Noticing and fixing one's own errors
**Era:** durable
**Guidepost:** The learner catches a mistake or misconception and revises it.

# D02 — Understanding & Sense-Making

**Question:** Did the learner build genuine understanding, or only move text around?

## D02.CONCEPT_GRASP
**Name:** Grasping a concept
**Era:** durable
**Guidepost:** The learner shows they understand what a concept means, not just its label.

## D02.MECHANISM
**Name:** Understanding a mechanism
**Era:** durable
**Guidepost:** The learner can explain how or why something works, step by step.

## D02.MISCONCEPTION_REPAIR
**Name:** Repairing a misconception
**Era:** durable
**Guidepost:** The learner moves from a wrong belief to a correct one, evidenced by the change.

## D02.OWN_WORDS
**Name:** Restating in one's own words
**Era:** AI-era
**Guidepost:** The learner reformulates an idea rather than transcribing it verbatim.

## D02.PRECISION
**Name:** Precision of expression
**Era:** durable
**Guidepost:** The learner states things exactly, distinguishing near-but-different ideas.

# D03 — AI Orchestration & Cognitive Control

**Question:** How did the learner use AI — as a tool for thinking, or as a substitute for it?

## D03.PROMPT_QUALITY
**Name:** Quality of questioning
**Era:** AI-era
**Guidepost:** The learner's prompts are specific, build on prior answers, and probe rather than fetch.

## D03.VERIFICATION
**Name:** Checking AI output
**Era:** AI-era
**Guidepost:** The learner tests, questions or confirms what the AI says rather than accepting it.

## D03.TRANSFER_VS_AUTHORSHIP
**Name:** Transcription vs authorship
**Era:** AI-era
**Guidepost:** The balance between text moved from the AI and text the learner composed themselves.

## D03.OFFLOADING
**Name:** Cognitive offloading
**Era:** AI-era
**Guidepost:** Whether the learner offloaded the thinking itself, not just the typing, to the AI.

## D03.INTEGRATION
**Name:** Integrating AI help into understanding
**Era:** AI-era
**Guidepost:** The learner turns AI assistance into their own consolidated understanding.
